# Quick Start Guide

Get started with the **Fake Name & Identity Generator** in three simple, friendly steps. You do not need any coding knowledge, software installation, or special permissions.

---

## 3 Simple Steps to Use the Tool

### Step 1: Open the Generator in Your Browser
Visit the tool on your computer, tablet, or smartphone browser (such as Google Chrome, Apple Safari, Microsoft Edge, or Mozilla Firefox). The generator loads immediately with a fully formed identity ready to use.

### Step 2: Choose Your Settings
Use the simple dropdown menus at the top to customize your generated profile:
- **Gender:** Select **All / Random**, **Male**, or **Female**.
- **Country:** Choose from 24 countries (such as United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Bangladesh, Germany, Australia, etc.).
- **Nameset:** Pick cultural and localized naming traditions.

Whenever you change a setting or click the blue **"Generate"** button, a brand-new persona is created instantly.

### Step 3: Copy or Download Your Details
- **Copy Anything in One Click:** Hover or tap on any information (such as Name, Street Address, Phone Number, or Test Card) and click the copy icon to instantly paste it into your form, test field, or document.
- **Save the Profile:** Click **"Save"** to keep a personal list of favorite profiles during your session.
- **Bulk Creation:** Need multiple profiles for testing? Click **"Bulk Generate"** at the top right, pick how many you need (up to 100), and download them as an easy-to-open spreadsheet (CSV) or text file.

---

## Helpful Tips
- **Profile Photos:** Click directly on the profile portrait to download a high-resolution image to your device.
- **Barcode & QR Codes:** Scroll down on any profile to see instantly generated UPC barcodes, QR codes, and tracking numbers ready for presentation mockups.
- **Dark Mode:** Tap the sun/moon toggle in the top bar anytime to switch between comfortable light and dark reading themes.
