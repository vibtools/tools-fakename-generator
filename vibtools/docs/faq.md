# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find clear, simple answers to common questions regarding pricing, privacy, compatibility, and product updates.

---

### 1. Pricing: Is this tool free to use?
**Yes, 100% free.**
There are no fees, subscriptions, paywalls, or hidden charges. You have unlimited access to individual generation, bulk generation, and file downloads without ever having to provide payment information or register an account.

---

### 2. Data Privacy: Are these real people? Is my data safe?
**All identities are completely synthetic and fictional.**
- **No Real Persons:** The names, phone numbers, and addresses are computer-generated synthetic combinations designed purely for testing and design mockups.
- **No Real Money:** Test credit cards use the mathematical Luhn formula for input verification only; they do not link to any financial institution, bank account, or funds.
- **Zero Data Logging:** We do not collect, track, or save your activity or generated data on our servers. Everything is created directly inside your device's web browser and resets when you leave.

---

### 3. Compatibility: What devices and browsers are supported?
The generator is fully compatible with all modern web browsers and hardware platforms:
- **Browsers:** Google Chrome, Apple Safari, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, Opera, and Brave.
- **Devices:** Works seamlessly on Windows PCs, macOS laptops, Linux workstations, Chromebooks, iPads, iPhones, and Android smartphones.
- **No Installation:** You do not need to install plugins, extensions, or run any software. It opens instantly in your web browser.

---

### 4. Updates: How do I get new updates and features?
**Updates are completely automatic.**
Because the application runs in the cloud, whenever our engineering team introduces new country databases, improved address algorithms, or visual improvements, they are delivered immediately the next time you visit the page. You never have to manually download, patch, or install updates.

---

### 5. Can I use these generated profiles for commercial mockups?
**Yes.**
You are free to use the generated synthetic information in UI/UX prototypes, app demonstrations, software testing, educational tutorials, quality assurance scenarios, and presentation screenshots without attribution or licensing hurdles.
